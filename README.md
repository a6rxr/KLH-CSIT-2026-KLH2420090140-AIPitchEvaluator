## Abstract

The Adaptive AI Powered Startup Pitch Evaluation and Investor Simulation Platform is an AI-driven system designed to analyze startup pitches and simulate investor evaluation. The platform uses artificial intelligence techniques to assess business ideas based on market potential, scalability, innovation, financial feasibility, and investor perspective. It provides automated feedback, scoring, and simulated investor interactions to help entrepreneurs improve their startup proposals.
